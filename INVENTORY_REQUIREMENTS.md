# Inventory / Fulfillment Requirements

- **REQ-INV-001** Available SHALL equal On Hand - Reserved for the selected branch/context
- **REQ-INV-002** Delivery SHALL NOT exceed remaining Sales Order quantity
- **REQ-INV-003** Delivery SHALL NOT exceed ready/available quantity when stock/production evidence is required
- **REQ-INV-004** Duplicate SKU/line rows SHALL be aggregated before quantity validation
- **REQ-INV-005** Mixed fulfillment SHALL support Stock + Production + Purchase without double counting
